# Breadboard Photos

This folder has pictures of the ALU while I was building it on breadboards.

The photos show the real circuit using logic ICs, DIP switches, jumper wires, LEDs, resistors, and capacitors.

These images are mainly here to document the physical build, not to act as a perfect wiring guide. Some parts may look messy because the circuit was tested and debugged step by step.

The breadboard version may also look different from the Logisim or TinkerCAD simulations because real wiring needs more space and practical adjustments.
